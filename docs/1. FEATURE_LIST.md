# AteThat — maintained feature list

## Current local refinement — Food Library and tracked nutrients

Food Library for individual reusable foods; Recipes for combinations (including older saved meals). Log food opens My foods, alongside Quick log and Search; all sources and fractional recipe portions can be combined before review. One-time manual logging has optional library saving and an explicit blank-visible-fields-as-zero choice. App-wide tracked nutrient selection, read-only account email, public wording cleanup and automatic seven-day Analysis are included in v2.8.0. [Scope, compatibility and verification](FOOD_LIBRARY_AND_TRACKED_NUTRIENTS.md). See [v2.8.0 release verification](RELEASE_2_8_0.md); emailed PDF reports remain Phase 3.

## Phase 2.5 — implemented locally, not released

Independent Settings targets, deliberate 4/4/9 calorie refresh, dismissible editing-session mismatch notice, approved missing-profile assumptions (175 cm / 70.4375 kg / Moderate), profile-versus-logged-weight separation, target revision history and calculation explanation page. Optional setup inputs remain non-blocking. Factual editable Text Summary on Dashboard/Analytics with full Copy, confirmed regeneration and unsaved-draft protection; no AI integration. Activity-based adult calculated defaults and reset with current/new preview, applicability confirmation and atomic revision history are implemented locally. No separate training toggle. Automatic initial proposals and source-range custom-value acknowledgements remain follow-ups. See [implementation status and verification](PHASE_2_5_STATUS.md).

## Phase 1 — shipped in v1.0.0

- Email registration/verification, login/reset, Google sign-in and explicit linking, session rotation, logout and verified account deletion/cancellation.
- Profile, DOB, timezone, daily reset, units/theme; Tracking Day and versioned general FDA benchmarks.
- USDA search/detail; private custom foods, favourites, recent/frequent foods, personal servings and reusable meals.
- Group preview, explicit idempotent confirmation, corrections and immutable nutrition snapshots. Unknown is not zero; canonical calculations belong to Core.
- Dashboard/history, basic daily/weekly health schedules/actions, responsive light/dark browser UI.
- Public API/private Core, MongoDB Atlas, VPS and Cloudflare frontend hosting.

## Phase 2 — shipped in v2.0.0

- Richer nutrient groups, supported vitamins/minerals/fat/amino-acid/fibre subtypes, trends, known-day averages, historical target comparisons and contribution drill-downs.
- Recipes with ingredients/states/quantities, preparation, yield, batch/per-serving nutrition, fractional portions and reviewed overrides. Saved meals load editable drafts.
- Water, weight and activity CRUD, canonical units, saved Tracking Day history/trends and optional owner-chosen water goal.
- Daily/weekly/monthly schedules, multiple times/month dates/end dates, versioned future-date pause/resume, history and adherence.
- DOB month/year selection, grouped nutrient entry, Terms recovery limitation and production fixes for paused schedules and B6/B12 identifiers.

Dedicated combined macro-distribution and protein-consistency score views are optional follow-ons, not shipped. Barcode/product lookup is removed entirely. Additional regional providers are not integrated and are not Phase 2 blockers.

## Phase 3 — planned

- Agreed addition: persistent local-first shared USDA catalogue, explicit discovery limited to 10 searches/user/day, bounded imports, upstream/storage safeguards and manual cleanup, using a separate logical MongoDB database without Redis.
- Original roadmap: label/image extraction, food-photo/natural-language input, voice-to-text, CSV/JSON exports and scoped external-agent/MCP integration.
- Emailed Analysis report with a PDF attachment for the applied timeline, nutrition and selected health items, offered through the explicit Explore flow. Automatic seven-day page loading does not send email; delivery controls remain a Phase 3 design detail.
- All assisted writes retain review/confirmation, ownership and immutable history; agent access requires revocable permissions and minimum audit records.

See [Phase 3 backlog](PHASE_3_BACKLOG.md). Providers, spend, upload retention and rollout order require approval before external processing.

## Phase 4 and later — deferred

Durable infrastructure, broader audit/event analytics, notifications, offline/PWA, extra identity providers and scaling are conditional [platform work](PHASE_4_BACKLOG.md). Recovery implementation and additional Indian-food providers need separate approval.

## Boundaries across all phases

No diagnosis, prescriptions, automatic dosage changes or AI medical recommendation engine. No pantry/inventory/expiry tracking or native-app commitment. Private food edits do not change the shared catalogue. Source refreshes and recipe edits never rewrite confirmed history.
