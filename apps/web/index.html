AteThat: food, nutrition and routine tracking

AteThat is a private daily tracker for food, nutrition, water, workouts and health routines.

Log food, review nutrition, track water and activity, and keep routines such as medication, supplements or personal tasks in one clear place.

Create an account or log in to your private dashboard.

What you can track

  • Food, recipes and nutrition details
  • Water intake, weight and workouts
  • Daily nutrition, calorie balance and activity trends
  • Personal routines, medication, supplements and tasks

AteThat is a tracking tool and does not provide medical advice.
